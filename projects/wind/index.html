About this map

Each line traces the wind’s path, sped up thousands of times so the motion is visible. The fastest winds are sped up least, so storms stay legible. Color shows wind speed.

The winds come from the Global Forecast System, the National Weather Service’s worldwide weather model, on a one‑degree grid (about 110 kilometers) prepared by earth.nullschool.net.

It is a forecast that shows broad patterns, not a measurement, so local winds can differ.

Named storms and their peak sustained winds come from the National Hurricane Center and Joint Typhoon Warning Center advisories issued when this forecast was made. The model’s coarse grid smooths a storm’s core, so a reading there shows less.
